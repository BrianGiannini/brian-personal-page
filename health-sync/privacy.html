health-sync privacy policy

health-sync is a personal tool used only by its author, Brian Giannini.

  • It requests read-only access to Google Health data, and only with the account owner's consent.
  • The data is stored on the author's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account settings (Security, Third-party apps and services).

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
